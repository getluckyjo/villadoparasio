# Vila do Paraíso — Brand & Website Project

> **Barefoot luxury on the edge of the Bazaruto.**
> Six private beach villas in Vilanculos, Mozambique. A butler and a chef at every door.
> One hour from Johannesburg. Intentionally simple, so nature does the talking.

---

## What this repository is

This is the **strategy, copy and design foundation** for the new Vila do Paraíso
website — the thinking that comes *before* a single line of site code. Everything
here is written to be handed to a designer, a copy editor, or a developer and used
immediately.

It is built around one idea: **Barefoot luxury — intentionally simple to let nature
shine.** Down-to-earth, not stiff. Warm, not corporate. A place where a barefoot
walk on the dunes and a chef-cooked dinner belong in the same sentence.

## The five things we lead with

1. **World-class fishing** — marlin, sailfish, kingfish, tuna in the Bazaruto channel.
2. **World-class snorkelling & diving** — Two Mile Reef, 200+ fish species, turtles, whale sharks.
3. **The ultimate family holiday** — whole-villa privacy, butler, chef, safe warm sea.
4. **One hour from Johannesburg** — barefoot, not far. Sundowners the day you leave home.
5. **Butler & chef included** — full board at a *reasonable* price, not an island fortune.

Plus the through-line: an **open door to the Bazaruto Archipelago** — island hopping,
dune picnics, dhow sunsets — straight off your own beach.

## The strategic shift this site is built for

Vila do Paraíso is moving **from self-catering to full board at an accessible price**,
and bringing **bookings online** — rooms, meals, *and* activities (fishing, diving,
snorkelling, island hopping, dune picnics, township food tours). The site has to sell
the experience *and* take the booking.

## References that shaped the direction

- **[Kisawa Sanctuary](https://kisawasanctuary.com/)** — the barefoot-luxury benchmark; low-key, unmanicured, natural, but stylised with a fierce eye for detail. We borrow the *restraint*, not the price tag.
- **[Perfect Hideaways](https://www.perfecthideaways.co.za/)** — editorial, owner's-voice storytelling and photography-led layouts. We borrow the *warmth and the story-first structure*.
- **[2M / Cerveja Dois M](https://www.cerveja2m.co.mz/)** — Mozambique's own beer, drunk at every braai and sunset since 1962. We borrow its *soul*: red, gold, the star, capulana colour, and unpretentious national pride — used as an artistic accent, never a costume.

## A note on the name

The official **logo** renders the name as **VILA DO PARAISO** (Portuguese, one "l", no
accent) with **MOÇAMBIQUE** beneath. English-speaking guests and some listings write
"Villa do Paraiso." Recommendation: **match the logo's spelling — "Vila do Paraiso" — in the
wordmark and headings**; the accented *Vila do Paraíso* is fine in flowing body copy; and
treat "Villa do Paraiso" as an SEO alias in metadata and alt text so we capture both searches.
See [`docs/02-development-strategy.md`](docs/02-development-strategy.md) for the SEO handling.
