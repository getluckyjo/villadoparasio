# 03 — Copywriting

Brand voice, plus ready-to-use copy for every page and the booking flow. Drop these
straight into the build and edit lightly. Placeholders in `{curly braces}` are facts the
owners must confirm (rates, names, schedules).

---

## 1. Voice & tone

**We sound like a well-travelled friend who happens to own the best beach house on the coast.**

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Warm, easy, human | Stiff, corporate, "we are delighted to…" |
| Vivid and specific (name the reef, the fish, the dish) | Vague adjective-soup ("luxurious, exclusive, world-class" on repeat) |
| Confident and quiet | Boastful or hard-selling |
| A little Mozambican in soul | A costume of other people's culture |
| Short. Plain. True. | Long, flowery, over-promised |

### Rules of thumb
- **Show, don't adjective.** Not "world-class fishing" alone — *"Marlin run the channel
  from October. Your skipper knows where."*
- **Lead with the guest, not us.** "You wake to…" beats "The villa offers…"
- **Earn 'luxury' by never saying it.** Let the butler, the chef, the empty beach prove it.
- **Sprinkle, don't smother:** a little Portuguese (*bem-vindo*, *vila*, *praia*), a nod to
  a cold 2M at sunset — used sparingly and with respect.
- **Barefoot, not careless.** Effortless for the guest = meticulous behind the scenes.

### Words we love / words we avoid
**Love:** barefoot, easy, warm, tide, dhow, reef, channel, deck, braai, sundowner, your
people, looked-after, the open door, paraíso.
**Avoid:** world-class (in body copy — fine as a pillar label), exclusive, opulent,
indulgent, pamper, nestled, oasis, "a stay to remember," "home away from home."
