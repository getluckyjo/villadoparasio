# 05 — Imagery, Sources & Moodboard

You asked to pull the best images of Vila do Paraíso, Vilanculos and the Bazaruto from the
web. Here's the honest, useful version of that: **where the real photography lives online**
(so you can collect, license or re-shoot it), a **photography shot-list** for the site, and a
**reference moodboard** for look & feel.

> ⚠️ **Licensing note (read this).** Almost every image of the property on OTAs and travel
> sites is **copyrighted** — by the property, by photographers, or by the platforms. I have
> **not** copied any images into this repo, because doing so without rights would expose the
> business to takedowns and legal risk. Treat the links below as a **collection and sourcing
> map**: (1) the property almost certainly already owns many of these — find the originals;
> (2) anything owned by an OTA or third party must be **licensed or re-shot** before it goes
> on the new site. The cleanest, most on-brand path is a **dedicated photo shoot** (Section 3).

## 3. Photography shot-list (the real solution)

The strongest, most defensible move is a **2–3 day shoot** at the property and on the water,
graded to one warm look (see [`04-look-and-feel.md`](04-look-and-feel.md)). Brief:

### Hero / property
- [ ] Villa exterior on the beach at **golden hour** (the signature shot)
- [ ] Private pool + deck + ocean, empty and inviting
- [ ] Bedroom (made, light, airy), en-suite detail
- [ ] Open-plan living/kitchen, indoor-outdoor flow
- [ ] Aerial/drone: the villas, the beach line, the archipelago beyond
- [ ] Detail texture set: thatch, weathered wood, linen, a cold **2M** beading with sweat

### The five pillars
- [ ] **Fishing:** the boat heading out at dawn; a marlin/sailfish moment; rods on the deck; the catch
- [ ] **Diving/snorkelling:** underwater reef + fish; kids snorkelling in the shallows; gear on the boat
- [ ] **Family:** kids mid-jump into the pool; multi-gen meal on the deck; barefoot on the sand
- [ ] **Getting here:** small plane / Vilanculos arrival; the transfer; "sand in shoes" first moment
- [ ] **Butler & chef:** chef plating on the deck; butler pouring sundowners; the laid table

### The open door (Bazaruto)
- [ ] Dhow under sail at sunset
- [ ] White sandbank appearing at low tide; figures tiny in a huge landscape
- [ ] **Dune picnic** set up on the great dunes, 360° blue
- [ ] **Township food tour:** market colour, grill smoke, matapa/peri-peri, real faces (with consent)
- [ ] **Sundowner ritual:** the warm-palette "2M moment"

### The table
- [ ] Fresh catch on ice; prawns; matapa & coconut rice; fruit at breakfast
- [ ] Beach braai under string lights; dinner on the dunes

**Shoot direction:** natural light, real people at ease (not models posing), wide & calm with
room for type, consistent warm grade. Get **model/property releases** so everything is clean
to publish and reuse forever.

## 5. Practical next steps on imagery

1. **Owners:** share the master photo folder + photographer details; confirm rights on the
   Booking.com gallery.
2. **Audit:** sort existing shots into "usable as-is", "re-grade", "re-shoot".
3. **Commission:** a 2–3 day shoot to fill the gaps in Section 3 — this is the single highest-
   impact spend for the new site.
4. **Stock top-up:** license area/wildlife/aerial shots from Adobe Stock/Getty for journal
   and atmosphere.
5. **Pipeline:** store full-res masters in the CMS (Sanity/Storyblok) → auto-served as
   responsive AVIF/WebP (see [`02-development-strategy.md`](02-development-strategy.md)).
